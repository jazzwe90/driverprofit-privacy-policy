1. Introduction

Welcome to DriverProfit.

DriverProfit is a driver earnings and trip profitability application designed to help e-hailing, ride-hailing and other drivers calculate estimated trip earnings, monitor expenses and track daily driving performance.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how DriverProfit handles information when you use our mobile application.

By using DriverProfit, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

DriverProfit allows users to enter information to calculate trip profitability and monitor their earnings.

Depending on how you use the application, information entered may include:

Trip Information
  • Offered trip fare.
  • Pickup distance.
  • Passenger journey distance.
  • Estimated return distance.
  • Platform commission.
  • Estimated trip expenses.
  • Trip acceptance or rejection status.
Driver Earnings Information
  • Daily income.
  • Net income.
  • Starting and ending mileage.
  • Fuel consumption.
  • Fuel expenses.
  • Working hours.
  • Average earnings.
Vehicle and Settings Information
  • Fuel price.
  • Vehicle fuel consumption.
  • Currency preferences.
  • Distance unit preferences.
  • Language preferences.
  • Other calculation settings.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information such as identity card numbers, bank account passwords or payment card details through the application's trip calculation features.

3. How Your Information Is Stored

DriverProfit is designed to store trip records, earnings information and application preferences locally on your device using device storage.

If information is stored locally, it is not automatically transmitted to a server operated by the developer.

You are responsible for maintaining your own device backups if you wish to preserve your information.

Uninstalling the application or clearing its storage may permanently remove locally stored information.

This does not necessarily delete information independently collected by third-party services.

4. How We Use Information

Information entered into DriverProfit is used to provide the application's features, including:

  • Calculating estimated trip profitability.
  • Estimating fuel expenses.
  • Calculating estimated net earnings.
  • Tracking accepted and rejected trips.
  • Monitoring daily driving activity.
  • Displaying trip history.
  • Saving user preferences.
  • Improving the application's functionality and user experience.

We do not sell users' locally stored trip or earnings records to third parties.
